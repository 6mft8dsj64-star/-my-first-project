2. Why we use personal information

  • Identifying members, sign-in and account management (including password reset and email verification)
  • Storing and syncing data so you can continue the same trip plans on multiple devices
  • Preventing misuse and responding to service errors
  • Reviewing and answering your messages, and improving the Service

We do not use personal information for advertising or marketing.

We process this information to the extent needed to conclude and perform the service agreement, under Article 15(1)4 of the Personal Information Protection Act. We do not collect sensitive information or unique identification numbers.

3. How long we keep it

Shared fares and daily spending are used, without your name or email, to build public fare statistics (typical price and range) that anyone can see. Each record also stores your unique account identifier to prevent duplicates. You can turn sharing off at any time with the checkbox under the price field, and your shared fares and daily spending are deleted when you delete your account.

We keep personal information until you delete your account and destroy it without delay when you do. You can delete your account yourself: after signing in, tap your profile at the top right, then Delete account.

Messages and reply emails are deleted within one year after the message is handled, or immediately on request. Only the operator can read messages.

Backups and access logs held by external services such as Firebase may remain for a period under those companies' policies before being deleted automatically.

4. How we destroy personal information

When you delete your account, your account information and travel data in the database are deleted immediately, together with that account's data stored in the browser you were using. Electronic files are deleted so that they cannot be recovered. We do not keep personal information on paper.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not provide your personal information to third parties, except where the law specifically requires it.

7. Browser storage

We use your browser's local storage (localStorage, sessionStorage) to remember your trip plans and sign-in state. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Deleting site data in your browser settings also deletes this information.

8. Your rights and how to exercise them

You can ask at any time to access, correct, delete or stop the processing of your personal information. You can change your name and trip plans directly in the Service, and delete all your information by deleting your account. For other requests, email the privacy officer below and we will handle them without delay.

You can also exercise these rights through a representative, such as a legal guardian or an authorized person, who must submit a power of attorney. We may check that the requester is you or your legitimate representative.

9. Security measures

  • All communication is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored encrypted by Firebase Authentication, and even the operator cannot see them.
  • Database security rules allow only you, when signed in, to read and write your own data.

10. Children under 14

The Service does not accept sign-ups from children under 14.
